## Abstract
While the Internet of Things (IoT) connects billions of physical sensors and actuators, raw telemetry alone rarely yields autonomous operational intelligence. The convergence of IoT and Artificial Intelligence—termed the Artificial Intelligence of Things (AIoT)—embeds machine learning inference directly across microcontrollers, edge gateways, and cloud clusters. This case study examines the architectural shift from cloud-centric IoT to a four-tier Edge AIoT reference model, quantifies end-to-end latency and bandwidth gains, analyzes three benchmark deployments (industrial predictive maintenance, adaptive smart-city traffic control, and smart-building HVAC microgrids), and evaluates key engineering solutions including `INT8` TinyML quantization and federated learning.
